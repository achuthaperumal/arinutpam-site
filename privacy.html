Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arinutpam Private Limited ("AriNutpam", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with our website arinutpam.ai (the "Site"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who we are

AriNutpam is an AI engineering and software consultancy based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. Our contact details are provided at the end of this policy.

2. Information we collect

The Site is primarily an informational marketing website. We collect limited information:

  • Information you provide. If you contact us through the enquiry form, by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to share — such as your name, email address, phone number, company and message.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting and network providers may automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability purposes.
  • Preferences. We store your light/dark theme choice locally in your browser (via local storage). This never leaves your device and is not used to identify you.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • respond to your enquiries and provide the information or services you request;
  • operate, secure and improve the Site; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated advertising profiling.

4. The in-browser AI demo

The Site includes an interactive AI demonstration that runs entirely within your own browser. Any image you use for the demo is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

5. Third-party services

To operate the Site we rely on a small number of trusted service providers:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the fonts used on the Site.
  • Google Maps — displays our location on the contact section.
  • Web3Forms — delivers enquiry-form submissions to our inbox.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of external websites we link to.

6. Cookies and local storage

The Site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your theme preference is kept in your browser's local storage. Some embedded third-party services (such as Google Maps or Google Fonts) may set their own cookies; you can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business records, and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us, and you may withdraw any consent you have given. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children

The Site is intended for a business audience and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
